FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers before you try it.

What is Scrybe?

Scrybe is an AI writing assistant that drafts, refines, and types essays directly inside Google Docs.

How does Scrybe work?

Paste your assignment prompt, choose a word count and academic level, and Scrybe writes, humanizes, and types the draft into your doc.

Is Scrybe free?

Yes — the free plan includes 2 essays a month up to 500 words each, plus 2 Type Text sessions a day. Paid plans start at $5/mo.

How much does Scrybe cost?

Free is $0, Pro is $5/mo (20 essays + 20 Type Text sessions a day), and Unlimited is $25/mo (200 essays, no word cap).

How do the plan limits work?

Essay limits reset monthly. Type Text session limits reset daily. Every plan includes rubric uploads, humanizing, and Google Docs export.

Does Scrybe work on Chromebooks?

Yes — Scrybe is a Chrome extension, so it works on Chromebooks, Windows, Macs, and any device that runs Chrome.

Does Scrybe support citation styles?

Yes — Scrybe supports APA, MLA, and Chicago and pulls real, verifiable references.

Is my data private?

Scrybe only stores your account and subscription info. Essay content is processed to create your draft but is not stored or shared. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — cancel anytime from your account page. No long-term commitment, no hidden fees.
