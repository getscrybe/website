Scrybe is an AI essay writer built for students who live in Google Docs. Instead of copying text out of a chat window, you give Scrybe your assignment and it writes the essay into the document itself — one character at a time, at a typing speed you control. The result looks and reads like you wrote it, because it arrives the same way your own typing would.

  • Writes from your prompt, rubric, word count, subject, and academic level
  • Humanizes the draft so it passes GPTZero and Turnitin

  • Optional outline step and a "request changes" pass before you finalize

How the AI essay writer works

1. Paste the assignment

Drop in your essay question and rubric. Choose the word count, subject, and grade level so the draft matches the brief.

2. Generate & refine

Scrybe writes a full draft and humanizes it. Ask for changes — a stronger intro, more evidence, a shorter conclusion — until it's right.

3. Set your pace

Choose how fast the essay types into the document, from a quick demo to a realistic multi-minute draft that lands naturally.

4. Watch it type

Open your Google Doc and hit start. Scrybe types the finished essay in, word by word, while you watch.
