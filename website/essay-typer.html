What is an auto typer?

An auto typer is a tool that enters text into a document character by character instead of pasting it all at once. Scrybe is a Chrome extension auto typer for Google Docs: paste text into the Type Text tab, choose a speed, and Scrybe types it into your open document using simulated keystrokes.

That makes it useful when you want the text to arrive progressively rather than as one large paste. Scrybe works with your own writing or text generated by an AI tool, and you can pause, resume, or stop at any point.

It also works as an essay typer: generate a draft with the AI essay writer, then type it into Google Docs.

  • Control the words-per-minute and target duration
  • Pause, resume, and stop at any point
  • Works in Google Docs on any Chrome device, including Chromebooks
