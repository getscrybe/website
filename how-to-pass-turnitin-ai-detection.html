How to pass Turnitin's
AI detection.

Detection flags patterns, not ideas. Here's what Turnitin looks for, how to write so you don't trip it, and where Scrybe fits in.

Turnitin's AI detector doesn't check whether you used AI. It estimates whether a passage looks machine-generated — and it reports a likelihood, not a verdict. That distinction matters, because it means the goal isn't to "beat" a scanner with tricks. It's to produce writing that genuinely reads human.

Note: whether AI assistance is allowed is up to your school's academic integrity policy. This guide is about writing that reads naturally.

What the detector actually looks at

Sentence length uniformity

AI tends to produce sentences of similar length. Human writing swings between short punches and longer, winding sentences.

Predictable transitions

"Furthermore," "Moreover," "In conclusion" in every paragraph is a tell. Humans connect ideas in messier, more varied ways.

Low specificity

Generic claims without concrete detail read as machine-made. Specifics — names, dates, examples — read human.

Statistical predictability

The core signal is how expected each next word is. Repetitive rhythm raises the score.

Five things that make an essay read human

  • Vary sentence rhythm. Mix short and long. Read it aloud and break anything that sounds sing-song.
  • Get specific. Replace "studies show" with an actual source, date, or example.
  • Cut filler transitions. Delete half the "moreover"s and let the argument carry the link.
  • Add your own voice. A sentence or two of genuine opinion or observation goes a long way.
  • Type instead of paste. A single giant paste is easy to spot in the document history.

Frequently asked questions

How does Turnitin's AI detector work?

It estimates how likely a passage was machine-generated by looking at word choice, sentence structure, and predictability, then reports a percentage rather than a yes/no result.

What does it flag as AI writing?

Uniform sentence length, predictable transitions, generic phrasing, and low variation in sentence construction. Human writing usually varies more.

How do I make an essay read human?
